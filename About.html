Founder & Visionary

Suresh Babu

Founder & Managing Director, Indus Paints

"A vehicle's paint is not merely a superficial coat; it is the ultimate expression of craft, pride, and engineering protection. When we started Indus Paints in 2007, our goal was simple: bring world-class paint chemistry and authentic application tools directly to technicians who refuse to compromise on quality."

Over nearly two decades, Mr. Suresh Babu has steered Indus Paints from a dedicated local mixing station in Vellore into one of Tamil Nadu's most respected authorized distributors for world-renowned paint titans — including Glasurit (BASF), Valspar, Kansai Nerolac, and Prospray.
